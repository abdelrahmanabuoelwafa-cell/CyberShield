Know your enemy

Common Digital Threats

These are the threats the topics on this site train you against. The risk column shows how much damage a successful attack typically causes for a normal user.

Threat What it is Main Target Risk
Phishing A fake email or website that impersonates a trusted brand to steal your login data. Credentials & card data High
Malware Harmful software (viruses, spyware, ransomware) installed through bad downloads or links. Devices & files High
Weak passwords Short, reused or guessable passwords that fall to automated cracking in minutes. Accounts High
Public Wi-Fi snooping Attackers on the same open network reading or modifying your unencrypted traffic. Traffic on open networks Medium
Data leaks Your data exposed through a service you use being breached; reuse then fuels account takeover. Personal data Medium

Quick wins

Start Today: Four Habits That Matter Most

  • Use a password manager. It creates a unique, random password for every account, so one leaked password never unlocks anything else.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). Even if your password is stolen, the attacker still needs your phone or authenticator app to get in.
  • Update everything, quickly. Most attacks exploit known bugs that were already fixed - updates close those doors.
  • Think before you click. Urgent language, strange links and unexpected attachments are the classic phishing pattern.
