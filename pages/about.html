About the project

Why We Built CyberShield

CyberShield started as the Multimedia course mini project for Team 6 at MTI. We chose the Cybersecurity Awareness topic because digital safety is the one skill every student, employee and family member uses every single day - and because it gave us strong images to edit in Adobe Photoshop.

The Idea

Most people think cybersecurity is a job for experts in dark rooms. In reality, the majority of successful attacks start with one small human mistake: a weak password, one rushed click on a fake link, or an unpatched app. Our goal with CyberShield is to translate the essential concepts - password strength, phishing detection, data protection and safe browsing - into plain language a non-technical visitor can read in minutes and apply the same day.

The website itself is also a technical exercise. It demonstrates structured HTML (semantic sections, navigation, lists, forms and tables), CSS design (a consistent visual identity, Flexbox layouts, reusable cards and buttons) and an Adobe Photoshop workflow with original and edited image versions, including a dedicated Before & After page.

Every image on this site has an original version kept intact and an edited version produced with Photoshop techniques such as crop & reframe, brightness/contrast, hue/saturation, gradient map duotone, curves and vignette. The layered PSD sources are delivered with the project so the editing steps can be inspected layer by layer.
